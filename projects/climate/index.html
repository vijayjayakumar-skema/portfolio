Case study · SKEMA Business School, Center of Sustainability · 03/2025 – 07/2025

Climate-data extraction, 110× faster

As Research Assistant, Vijay automated the extraction of daily climate data used to study how climate variation has affected vineyards’ business model.

~110×faster than manual extraction
5,500+locations across France, 1950–2024
~6 monthsearlier project delivery

Problem

The study needed daily minimum temperature, maximum temperature and precipitation for 75 years across 5,500+ locations: 35GB+ of NetCDF files. Manual extraction managed about 5 locations a day.

What was built

  • Multithreaded R scripts that extract and analyse the NetCDF data automatically
  • Validation of the extracted values against QGIS
  • QGIS geospatial analysis combining digital elevation and climate data
  • Excel and R Shiny dashboards to track survey distribution and responses

Result

5,500 locations processed in 10 days instead of ~5 a day, bringing the project forward by about six months.
