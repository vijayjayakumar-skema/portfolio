Case study · ZiA Distribution, France · 08/2025 – 07/2026

Retail POS for a paper-based depot

As freelance Consultant Product Manager, Vijay replaced a paper-based billing and stock process with an offline-friendly POS, built for staff with little or no computer experience.

7,500+transactions over 8 months
~700products managed in one depot
5daily users, trained hands-on

Problem

Billing and stock were tracked on paper. The owner had no view of stock movements, could not reorder before stock-outs, and could not see sales away from the depot.

What was built

  • Billing and invoice creation, payment-method updates and three pricing logics
  • Product and stock catalogues, stock-code creation and bill of materials (BOM) creation
  • Automated daily and monthly sales dashboards and report extraction
  • 3-character product prediction so non-technical staff can find items fast
  • CRM and ERP-style workflows added progressively from user feedback

Result

Full visibility of stock movements, reorders before stock-outs, and remote sales monitoring for the owner. The team used the tool day to day until the mission ended in 07/2026, when the parent company was liquidated.
